      How a project runs
      
      
      A simple, predictable process so you always know what's happening and what's next.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
      01
      Talk it through
      We go over your goals, pages, platform and examples you like, then agree on a clear scope.
      
      
      02
      Plan the design
      Layout and design direction are settled before the build starts, so there are no surprises.
      
      
      03
      Build and review
      I build in stages on a preview link you can check on desktop and mobile and comment on.
      
      
      04
      Launch and hand over
      We go live, and I walk you through updating content so the site stays yours to run.
